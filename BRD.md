# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 1. Project Overview

The project is a personal portfolio website that represents my professional identity and digital presence.

The website will introduce my educational background, skills, training, projects, achievements, and contact information in a simple and professional way.

## 2. Business Objective

The main objective is to create a professional online presence that can be shared with recruiters, hiring managers, and potential employers.

The website should present my background and career interests clearly and make it easy for visitors to understand my profile.

## 3. Target Audience

The primary target audience includes:

- Recruiters
- Hiring Managers
- Potential Employers
- Companies looking for fresh graduates

## 4. Professional Identity

The website should present me as a Business Administration graduate with a growing technical background and an interest in Data Analysis and Business Intelligence.

The website should reflect a professional, organized, and learning-oriented identity.

## 5. Positioning

The website positions me at the intersection of business and technology, combining a Business Administration background with developing technical skills in Data Analysis and Business Intelligence.

## 6. Business Goals

The website should:

- Introduce my professional background.
- Present my education and academic achievements.
- Showcase my technical and business-related skills.
- Display relevant training and projects.
- Provide an easy way to contact me.
- Build a professional digital presence.

## 7. Website Requirements

The website should contain the following main sections:

### Home

A clear introduction including my name, professional background, and career interest.

### About Me

A brief description of my educational background and professional interests.

### Skills

A list of relevant technical and business skills.

### Education

My university/institute education and academic achievement.

### Training & Certifications

Relevant training programs, certifications, and activities.

### Projects

A section for presenting relevant projects.

### Contact

Professional contact information, including email and LinkedIn.

## 8. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

- Visitors can understand my professional background quickly.
- My main skills and interests are clearly presented.
- My education, training, and projects are easy to find.
- Visitors can access my contact information.
- The website has a clean and professional structure.
- The website is accessible through GitHub Pages.
