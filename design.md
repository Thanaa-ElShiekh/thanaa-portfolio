# Personal Portfolio Website Design

## 1. Project Overview

A personal portfolio website designed to introduce myself, showcase my skills, education, training, certificates, and projects.

The website represents my professional identity and career interests in Data Analysis and Business Intelligence.

## 2. Target Audience

The portfolio is designed for:

* Recruiters
* Hiring Managers
* Companies
* Potential Employers

## 3. Website Goals

* Introduce my professional background.
* Showcase my technical and business skills.
* Display my projects and achievements.
* Provide an easy way to contact me.
* Present my career interests in a professional way.

## 4. Website Sections

### Home

A short introduction including my name, professional background, and career interest.

### About Me

A brief overview of my educational background and professional interests.

### Skills

The main skills to be displayed include:

* SQL
* Power BI
* Microsoft Excel
* Microsoft Access
* Odoo ERP
* Data Analysis
* Business Intelligence

### Projects

A section to showcase academic, data analysis, and other relevant projects.

### Education

Bachelor of Business Administration.

### Training & Certifications

A section for relevant training programs, certificates, and achievements.

### Contact

The website will include links to my professional contact information, including LinkedIn and email.

## 5. Design Principles

* Clean and professional layout.
* Simple and clear navigation.
* Easy-to-read typography.
* Responsive design for different screen sizes.
* Consistent spacing and structure.
* Professional presentation suitable for recruiters.

## 6. User Experience

The website should allow visitors to quickly understand who I am, what skills I have, what projects I have worked on, and how they can contact me.
